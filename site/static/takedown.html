Takedown policy

This is a small hobby archive of mostly anonymous posts, run by Hiina. It is not 4chan, and 4chan's own rules are not applied here.

How to ask

Email hiina@hiina.space with the post number (No.123456789), a link to the post (archive.vrg.party/p/?123456789), or the thread number. Say whether you want the whole post removed or only its image.

What gets removed

  • Illegal content. The post number is enough; it is removed as soon as possible.
  • Private personal information about you that is not otherwise public, such as a real name, address, ID numbers or photos. Things you chose to share publicly elsewhere, such as an online handle, don't count. If a post puts you at risk in some other way, explain why and it will be considered case by case.
  • Copyrighted material used beyond fair use. Say what the work is and that you own it or act for the owner.

Since almost every post is anonymous, the archive can't confirm who wrote one, so requests are judged on the reason given, not on claimed authorship. For personal-information or copyright requests, give enough detail to decide, and a way to reply if there are questions.

What happens

  • You get a reply within 3 days.
  • Removed posts disappear from search, thread pages, permalinks and thumbnails at the next build. Illegal content and other urgent cases are rebuilt by hand within 3 days.
  • Removing an image removes it from every post that carries the same file; the post text can stay.
  • Removals are permanent and are also left out of any data export made afterwards.

Limits

  • This only removes things from archive.vrg.party. 4chan, b4k and other archives are separate.
  • Replies that quoted a removed post still show its number (>>123456789) as plain text, without its content.
  • Search engines may keep a copy of a thread page until they recrawl it; Google's outdated-content removal tool can speed that up.
