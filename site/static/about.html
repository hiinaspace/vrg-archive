About this archive

What this is

A searchable archive of the 4chan /vg/ /vrg/ (Virtual Reality General) threads, kept for people who want to find an old post, link, or discussion. It is an unofficial hobby project, not affiliated with 4chan, b4k, or any poster. It is not a mirror of 4chan, not a live board, and not a place to post; it does not serve full-size images and it is not a complete record: some posts and thumbnails are missing (see below).

What is indexed

__POSTS__ posts in __THREADS__ threads, __FIRST__ to __LAST__ (UTC). Data as of __SNAP__.

Sources and credit. Posts come from the 4chan API (2025-04 onward), from an earlier archive export of the board (before that), and from b4k (arch.b4k.dev), which supplied posts and thumbnails the other sources missed. Thanks to the 4chan archivists, to b4k, and to the author of the earlier export. Post text and thumbnails belong to their posters and sources; this site only indexes them.

Shown: post text, post number, time, poster name or tripcode if any (almost all are Anonymous), file name and dimensions, and small thumbnails. Not shown: full-size images or video, IP addresses, or any poster identifier beyond what 4chan itself published (/vg/ has no poster IDs). Thumbnails are the small previews 4chan or b4k published; spoilered ones stay blurred until clicked, where the source recorded the spoiler. Most posts from before 2019-08 show a placeholder with the file name and dimensions instead.

Deleted posts are excluded everywhere: not in the search index, not on thread pages, not in quote previews, and posts whose file was deleted get no thumbnail. Deletion flags for 2019-08 to 2025-04 come from b4k; posts from before 2019-08, and the few b4k no longer has, carry no deletion flag, so a post deleted on 4chan can still appear here; ask for a takedown below. A >>N that points at a missing or excluded post is shown as plain text.

Refresh

Rebuilt from time to time (a weekly rebuild is planned). Only threads whose last post is more than 7 days old are published, so moderator deletions have settled first.

Privacy

The site is static files with no search server and no accounts; search runs in your browser, and your query lives only in the page URL. The site itself keeps no logs and sets no cookies. It is served by Cloudflare Pages, which, like any host, may process request metadata (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy; we do not receive it. Thread pages may be indexed by search engines; the search page and index files are marked noindex.

Takedowns

Posts and images can be removed for illegal content, private personal information, or copyright. Email hiina@hiina.space with the post number (No.123456789; the number is in the page URL and /p/?N links). Removals take effect at the next build; illegal content and other urgent cases are rebuilt by hand within 3 days. Full details: takedown policy. Removal here does not remove anything from 4chan or b4k.

Data export

The old version of this site offered a downloadable DuckDB/Parquet dump of the archive. A data export of the published posts (what this site shows, without removed or deleted posts) is available on request: contact hiina@hiina.space.

Search syntax

quest proall words (case-insensitive; digits count; plurals are folded)
"exact phrase"words next to each other
-word, a | b, (a | b) cexclude, either, grouping. OR binds tighter than a space
thread:1978one thread (edition number or thread id)
after:2023-05 before:2024date range; date:2023, date:2023-01..2023-06, date:>=2024
has:image, ext:png, has:linkimage posts, file type (jpg png gif webm mp4), posts with links
op:yesfirst posts of threads
replies:>=5posts with at least that many replies
domain:youtube.composts linking a site (the commonest sites only)
name:foo, trip:abc123poster name or tripcode (almost all posts are Anonymous)
filename:vrchatwords in the posted file's name
greentext:implyingonly inside >greentext lines
>>585933859replies to that post

Japanese, Chinese and Korean text is matched as character pairs. Very common words (the, a, to ...) are ignored when the query has other words. Quote numbers are not searchable as words. Facet counts cover every post matching the query and all filters.
